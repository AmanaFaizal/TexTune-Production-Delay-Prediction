# How I Used AI in This Project

I used **Claude** as a learning and coding assistant while working on the TexTune Production Order Delay Prediction assignment.

### How AI Helped Me

- **Understanding the data:** AI helped me understand missing values, class imbalance, and data preprocessing.
- **Writing code:** AI helped me write and improve Python code for EDA, feature engineering, model training, and evaluation.
- **Choosing models:** I discussed Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and CatBoost to understand their differences.
- **Model evaluation:** AI explained precision, recall, F1-score, cross-validation, and threshold tuning.
- **Feature engineering:** I explored whether the planned start date could provide useful information for prediction.
- **Improving explanations:** AI helped me organize my observations and improve the English in my notebook.

### Suggestions I Questioned

I questioned whether CatBoost was always better than XGBoost and whether manually selected hyperparameters were enough. AI helped me understand why model comparison and proper evaluation are important.

### My Contribution

I explored the dataset, compared different approaches, reviewed the results, and checked the code and explanations. I also asked AI to explain things I didn't understand instead of simply accepting its suggestions.

AI was a helpful learning tool, but I focused on understanding my decisions and being able to explain my work.
